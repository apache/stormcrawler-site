Chain of trust

You must verify the integrity of the downloaded files: every release ships with everything you need to prove it came from us. The chain has four links:

01 · GET

Source archive

Fetch the tar.gz from an Apache mirror.

TAR.GZ

02 · CHECK

Integrity

Your SHA-512 checksum must match ours.

SHA512

03 · PROVE

Authenticity

The OpenPGP signature (*.asc) is made by a release manager.

ASC

04 · TRUST

Key ring

Match the signature against the project's KEYS file.

KEYS

Verify like a release manager

$ sha512sum -c apache-stormcrawler-{{ site.version }}-source-release.tar.gz.sha512
$ gpg --import KEYS
$ gpg --verify apache-stormcrawler-{{ site.version }}-source-release.tar.gz.asc
